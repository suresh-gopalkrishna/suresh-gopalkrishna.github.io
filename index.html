    Featured open-source project

    BoilFlux

    Open inverse heat transfer for boiling and quenching. BoilFlux reconstructs the transient, spatially resolved heat flux on a quenched, moving hot plate from infrared thermography of its back face, using a nonlinear 3D model and the adjoint conjugate-gradient method. It includes boiling-regime analysis, a neural-operator prototype for boiling flow, and benchmarks with known ground truth.
