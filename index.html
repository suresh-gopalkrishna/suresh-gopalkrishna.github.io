PROFILE

Physics, computation, and validation.

I am a computational scientist with approximately eight years of experience in multiphysics simulation and thermal analysis. My work combines finite-element and CFD-style modelling with laboratory experiments, inverse heat-transfer methods, and scientific machine learning.

I am particularly interested in physics-informed neural networks (PINNs) and surrogate models that make high-fidelity engineering simulations faster and more useful for real-world decisions.

CORE EXPERTISE

From thermal experiments to predictive models.

Thermal simulation

Transient thermal and thermomechanical modelling using ANSYS Mechanical, ANSYS Fluent, COMSOL Multiphysics and STAR-CCM+.

Inverse problems & ML

Inverse heat conduction, Physics-Informed Neural Networks, data-driven modelling, and surrogate-model concepts in Python and TensorFlow.

Experimental validation

Infrared thermography, transient temperature measurement, test-set-up development, and rigorous model–experiment comparison.
