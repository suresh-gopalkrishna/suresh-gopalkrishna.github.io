About

I am a computational scientist with about eight years of experience in multiphysics simulation and thermal analysis, including inverse heat transfer problems and scientific machine learning in Python/TensorFlow. I have combined FEM and CFD-style models with laboratory experiments, using numerical methods and data-driven approaches to understand and improve complex thermal processes. I am now particularly interested in developing physics-informed surrogate models with PINNs to accelerate high-fidelity simulations in demanding engineering applications.
